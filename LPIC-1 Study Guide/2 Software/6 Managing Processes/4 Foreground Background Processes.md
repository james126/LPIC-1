### Foreground and Background Processes
Normally when you launch a program it takes over the terminal. <br>
* ```Ctrl+Z``` pauses the program and gives you back the terminal <br>
* ```fg``` restores the program to your terminal
<br>
<br>
* ```bg``` keeps a program running in the background and gives your terminal back <br>
* You can use ```Ctrl+Z``` to give you back the terminal, then ```bg``` to run a program in the background <br>
