# Managing Runlevel Services

> #### chkconfig
> list services and runlevels
> `chkconfig options`
> |||
> |-|-|
> | `--list` | list all services as on/off and runlevels |
> | `--list [service-name]`<br> **--list http** | show services as on/off and runlevel |
> | `[service-name] on`<br> **http on**| enable service to start with default runlevel |
> | `--level level [service-name] on`<br> **--level 23 nfs-common on** | enable with runlevels | 
> |`--add [service-name]` | add service |
