### Firmware
* Lowest level software that runs on a computer <br>
* Begins the boot process and configures hardware devices <br>
<br>
* The most important firmware is installed on the computers motherboard  <br>
* In the past most *x86* and *x86-64* computers used firmware called *Basic Input/Output System (BIOS)* <br>
Since 2011, a new type of firmware known as *Extensible Firmware Interface (EFI)* or *Unified EFI (UEFI)* has become standard <br>
<br>
The motherboards firmware resides in flash memory
